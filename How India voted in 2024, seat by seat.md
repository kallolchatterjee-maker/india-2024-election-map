# How India voted in 2024, seat by seat

All 543 Lok Sabha constituencies, with each seat’s votes piled up as grains of sand. Scroll to see who won where, and how many votes sit behind each result.

Results: Election Commission of India, constituency-wise 2024 (ge2024byPC.xlsx). Boundaries: DataMeet parliamentary constituencies, simplified, CC BY-SA 2.5 India. These are the 2019 boundaries, which do not reflect Assam’s 2023 redrawing. Assam seats and Anantnag are matched to the nearest old constituency by name and are approximate. Orange and blue grains count votes for the first- and second-placed candidate of each alliance in a seat. Grey is all other candidates, worked out from each winner’s vote share. Alliances follow the post-election count (NDA 293, INDIA bloc 232, others 18). Surat was elected unopposed and has no pile.
