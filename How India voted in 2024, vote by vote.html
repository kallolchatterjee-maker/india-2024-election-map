What the source data can show

Sources: data/india_pc_2019_simplified.geojson, data/lok_sabha_2024_constituency_winners.csv, data/lok_sabha_2024_state_seats.csv, and data/lok_sabha_2024_party_results.csv.

Election values are shown as present in the local CSVs. Only a BJP winner is colored orange; all other constituencies remain neutral. Neutral coloring does not imply a missing result.
